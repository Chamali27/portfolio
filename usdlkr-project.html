// Project Overview

This research paper investigates the dominance of the US Dollar and its economic impact on developing countries specifically Sri Lanka. A CRISP-DM machine learning pipeline was developed to analyze 16 years of daily LKR/USD exchange rate data obtained from the Central Bank of Sri Lanka (May 2010 - February 2026), comparing Random Forest, XGBoost, and Gradient Boosting models, followed by an ensemble approach. The study provides 30-day forward predictions with 95% confidence intervals and delivers policy-relevant insights.

Team: WACI Abeysekara  ·  RL Hewanayaka  ·  MZM Hussein  ·  MN Mohamed
Presented at: ADScAI 2026 · University of Moratuwa · April 2026

The study traces dollar dominance from the 1944 Bretton Woods Agreement through the Nixon Shock (1971), the petrodollar agreement with Saudi Arabia (1974-s2024), and its ongoing implications for developing economies like Sri Lanka. The 2022 Sri Lankan economic crisis triggered by compounding effects of the Easter attacks (2019), COVID-19 (2020-21), political instability, and poor fiscal decisions is presented as a case study in how dollar dependency amplifies economic vulnerability.

// Methodology (CRISP-DM)

01
Data Understanding

3,346 LKR/USD observations (2010-2026). ADF test confirmed non-stationarity; 2022 crisis identified as a key shift.

02
Feature Engineering

14 features including moving averages, volatility, and lag-based indicators to capture trends and momentum.

03
Modeling

Random Forest, XGBoost, and Gradient Boosting trained with a weighted ensemble approach.

04
Evaluation

Performance measured using MAPE, R², MAE, and RMSE on unseen test data.

05
Forecasting

30-day recursive forecasting with confidence intervals for risk estimation.

// Three Phases of LKR/USD Exchange Rate (2010–2026)

PHASE 01 · 2010-2020
Stable Depreciation
110 → 200 LKR/USD at ~6.2% annual depreciation rate. Gradual, predictable decline.
PHASE 02 · 2022 CRISIS
80% Crash
Spike to 365 LKR/USD within months. Caused by tax cuts, import restrictions, and political collapse.
PHASE 03 · 2023-2026
IMF Recovery
Stabilization at 300-310 LKR/USD following IMF intervention and structural adjustment programs.

// Key Insights

XGBoost achieved best performance (MAPE: 0.838%, R²: 0.904).

Lag_1, Lag_7, and MA_30 were the strongest predictors.

🌍
USD remains dominant in global trade despite recent shifts.

Ensemble model (MAPE: 0.94%) is recommended for production use.
