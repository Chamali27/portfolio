// Project Overview

This project analyzes tourist arrivals to Sri Lanka using official data from the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA). The study focuses on post-COVID recovery, country-level demand, and seasonal tourism patterns between 2020 and 2025. Interactive Power BI dashboards were developed to support tourism planning and data-driven decision-making.

// Key Questions Explored

How did Sri Lanka's tourism sector recover after COVID-19?
Which countries contribute the most tourist arrivals?
What seasonal patterns affect tourism demand?
How dependent is Sri Lanka on specific regions?

// Methodology

01
Data Collection

Collected official tourism data from SLTDA reports and monthly Excel datasets (2020–2025).

02
Data Preparation

Manually extracted and validated data, consolidating 13,500+ records into a structured Excel dataset.

03
Data Modelling

Designed fact and dimension tables including country, region, and time dimensions for analysis.

04
Dashboard Development

Built interactive Power BI dashboards with KPIs, slicers, and trend analysis visuals.

// Key Insights

📉
Tourism dropped drastically during COVID-19 (2020–2021) but showed strong recovery from 2022 onwards.
🚀
A 270% growth in 2022 marked one of the fastest tourism recoveries in the region.
🌏
India remained the #1 source market throughout all years, dominating arrivals consistently.
🌍
Europe contributes over 50% of total arrivals, highlighting strong dependence on one region.
📅
December is the peak tourism month, while May consistently records the lowest arrivals.
⚠️
High seasonality creates operational risks, suggesting the need for off-season tourism strategies.
