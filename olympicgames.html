// Project Overview

This project analyzes all-time Olympic Games medal data across both Summer and Winter Olympics. Using Power BI, the team developed an interactive dashboard to explore country performance, medal distribution, and participation trends. The analysis highlights global sporting dominance and long-term success patterns across more than 100 nations.

Team: WACI Abeysekara  ·  RL Hewanayaka  ·  MZM Hussein  ·  MN Mohamed

// Key Questions Explored

Which countries dominate the Summer vs. Winter Olympics?
How are medals distributed across gold, silver, and bronze?
Is there a relationship between participation and success?
Which countries perform consistently across both Olympic seasons?

// Methodology

01
Data Collection

Collected Olympic medal data from Wikipedia's all-time medal table for both Summer and Winter Games.

02
Data Cleaning

Handled missing values, standardized country names using IOC codes, and formatted all numerical fields.

03
Data Structuring

Combined Summer and Winter datasets into a unified structure to enable comparative cross-season analysis.

04
Dashboard Development

Built Power BI dashboards with interactive filters, KPI cards, and comparative medal visualizations.

// Key Insights

🏆
The United States, China, and Russia dominate the Summer Olympics with the highest all-time medal counts.
❄️
Norway and Germany lead the Winter Olympics, excelling consistently in specialized winter sports disciplines.
⚖️
Countries with balanced medal distributions across gold, silver, and bronze tend to show the most consistent performance.
📊
A strong correlation exists between long-term Olympic participation history and total medal success over time.
🌍
Countries like Germany and Canada perform strongly in both Summer and Winter Olympics, showcasing sporting versatility.
🎯
Strategic investment in specific sports leads to concentrated medal success, particularly for smaller nations.
